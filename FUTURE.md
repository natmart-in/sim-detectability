# Future work (v2 ideas)

- **Level-of-detail physics knob.** Deliberately out of scope for v1 — the village
  has no physics rich enough to make it meaningful. A v2 world with continuous
  quantities (weather simulation, mechanics, fluid levels) could coarsen its
  update rules when unobserved and let the investigator hunt for discretisation
  seams.
- **Multi-investigator societies.** Several investigators with different priors who
  can share (or hoard) evidence; measures social epistemology of anomaly hunting.
- **Epistemic spread.** Let Descartes publish findings to the other villagers and
  observe belief propagation, resistance, and the sociology of "simulation talk".
- **Adversarial simulator.** A simulator agent that watches the investigator and
  patches leaks reactively — an arms race rather than fixed conditions.
